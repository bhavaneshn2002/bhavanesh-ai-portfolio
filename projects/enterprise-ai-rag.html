What I built.

A full-stack RAG platform enabling authenticated users to upload PDF, DOCX and TXT documents and query them in natural language.

  • Text extraction and recursive chunking
  • 384-dimensional Sentence Transformer embeddings
  • ChromaDB vector search with user-specific retrieval
  • Source-grounded answers using Google Gemini
  • JWT authentication and bcrypt password hashing
  • PostgreSQL persistence and per-user document ownership
