What I built.

An end-to-end forensic pipeline designed to detect audio deepfakes, localize suspicious segments and produce explainable verification reports.

  • Bimodal CNN + BiLSTM architecture for audio deepfake detection
  • Grad-CAM and saliency maps for suspicious-segment localization
  • Temporal analysis of suspicious audio regions
  • Speech-to-text integration
  • Wikipedia-based fact verification for forensic reports
